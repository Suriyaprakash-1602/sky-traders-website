Our Export Process

From farm harvesting to container port dispatch — precision in every step.

STEP 01

Farm Collection

Selected mature coconuts picked directly from contracted coconut plantations in Udumelpet.

STEP 02

Cleaning & De-husking

Skilled labor removes outer fibrous husk according to buyer specification (Semi or Fully husked).

STEP 03

Grading & Weight Sorting

Automated and manual weight inspection to separate nuts into 500g+, 600g+ export categories.

STEP 04

Quality Check

Inspection for shell integrity, sound water content, and phytosanitary compliance clearance.

STEP 05

Export Packing

Packed into high-durability 25kg mesh or PP bags to ensure maximum airflow during transit.

STEP 06

Container Stuffing

Loaded into 40ft High Cube sea containers at our export warehouse with temperature/humidity controls.

STEP 07

Global Shipping

Vessel departure from Tuticorin / Chennai port to destination ports worldwide with full shipping documents.
